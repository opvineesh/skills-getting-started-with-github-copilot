# OctoAcme Project Management Knowledge Base

This `docs/` knowledge base centralizes OctoAcme's project management practices so teams can find consistent, searchable, and versioned guidance in one place. It captures decisions, process rationale, and reusable workflows to improve onboarding, reduce single-person dependency risk, and support repeatable project delivery.

## Why this knowledge base exists

- Consolidate previously scattered project management knowledge
- Turn tacit team know-how into explicit, reusable documentation
- Ensure equal access to process context for all team members
- Standardize and continuously improve workflows over time

## OctoAcme project management approach

OctoAcme uses a lifecycle approach that moves through **Initiation**, **Planning**, **Execution & Tracking**, **Release & Deployment**, and **Retrospective & Continuous Improvement**. Across all phases, the team emphasizes:

- **Customer-first outcomes**
- **Iterative delivery**
- **Clear ownership and accountability**
- **Data-informed decision making**
- **Psychological safety and healthy collaboration**

## Core roles

- **Project Manager:** Coordinates delivery, milestones, communication, and risk management.
- **Product Manager:** Defines value, priorities, and roadmap alignment with customer needs.
- **Developers:** Implement solutions, estimate work, and maintain technical quality.
- **QA:** Validates quality, test coverage, and release readiness.
- **Stakeholders:** Provide direction, constraints, and decision input at key checkpoints.

## Major artifacts and checklists

Key artifacts include project charter/context, scope and schedule plans, sprint/task tracking, risk and communication logs, release readiness and deployment checklists, and retrospective action plans. These artifacts create traceability from decisions to execution and help teams improve between cycles.

## Process documents in `docs/`

- **Project Management Overview** – End-to-end operating model, governance, and lifecycle summary.
- **Roles & Personas** – Responsibilities, decision boundaries, and collaboration expectations.
- **Initiation** – Intake, objectives, scope framing, and project kickoff criteria.
- **Planning** – Work breakdown, estimation, dependencies, milestones, and success metrics.
- **Execution & Tracking** – Delivery cadence, status reporting, issue management, and progress controls.
- **Risk & Communication** – Risk identification/mitigation and stakeholder communication routines.
- **Release & Deployment** – Release readiness gates, deployment process, and stabilization steps.
- **Retrospective & Continuous Improvement** – Reflection process, lessons learned, and follow-through actions.
